# Module 1 — Warehouse vs. Lake vs. Lakehouse (2-3 hrs)

**What you'll learn:** The three storage paradigms and why lakehouse won out for finance use cases that need both raw historical data and reliable, fast queries.

**Why it matters:** This is the single architectural decision underlying your whole product. You'll hear "lakehouse" in every vendor pitch — know what problem it's actually solving.
