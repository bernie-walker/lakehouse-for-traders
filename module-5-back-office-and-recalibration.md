# Module 5 — Back Office Data and Model Recalibration (3-4 hrs)

**What you'll learn:** The trade lifecycle from execution to settlement, why front-office and back-office records must reconcile, and the concept of "slowly changing dimensions" — how you version reference data over time instead of just overwriting it.

**Why it matters:** This directly maps to two things in your brief: "back office data on trades executed" (lifecycle + reconciliation) and "re-calibration of data models over time" (versioning/point-in-time correctness). This is the module most non-technical PMs get wrong, so it's worth extra time.
