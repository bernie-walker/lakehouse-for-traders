# Capstone Exercise

Using Modules 1-8, draft a one-page brief covering your actual project:

1. Which sources are exchange feeds vs. vendor feeds vs. internal, and what's known/unknown about each one's format and SLA (Module 2)
2. Which data needs real-time vs. batch, and why (Module 3)
3. A sketch of the business layer — what are the "facts" (trades, prices) and "dimensions" (instrument, counterparty, book) traders will query (Module 4)
4. How back-office trade data will reconcile against front-office records, and how model recalibration/versioning will be handled (Module 5)
5. What lineage/governance questions compliance will likely ask (Module 6)
6. A one-paragraph description of the target architecture in your own words (Module 7)

This brief is both the test of whether the concepts landed and a genuinely useful artifact — it's close to what you'll need to hand engineering as a requirements doc.
